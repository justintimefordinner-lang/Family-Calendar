# Privacy Policy

Family Calendar is open-source, self-hosted software. There is no hosted service and no
company collecting data.

- The software runs entirely on hardware you own (for example a Raspberry Pi on your home network).
- Google account access is read-only (`calendar.readonly`, `userinfo.email`) and is used solely to
  display calendar events on your own screen. Tokens and events are stored in a local SQLite
  database on your device and are never transmitted anywhere else.
- Weather data is fetched from Open-Meteo using only the location you configure.
- No analytics, telemetry, advertising, or third-party sharing of any kind.
- To revoke access, remove the account in the app's settings and/or at
  <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

Questions: open an issue on this repository.
